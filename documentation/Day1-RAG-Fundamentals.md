1. Introduction – Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Examples include answering questions, solving problems, understanding languages and making decisions.

2. Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs are AI models trained with large amounts of text and information to understand and respond to human questions. Examples include ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude.

3. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is a technique that allows AI to retrieve information from external sources before generating an answer. This helps the AI provide more accurate and relevant responses.

4. Why RAG Is Important

RAG is important because AI models do not always have the latest or specific information needed to answer certain questions. It allows AI to access external documents, databases and company information to provide better answers.

5. RAG Architecture – Seven Stages

=>The seven major stages of a RAG system are:

*Data Collection: Gathering information from documents, websites and other sources.

*Data Processing: Cleaning and preparing the collected information.

*Chunking: Breaking large documents into smaller sections.

*Embedding: Converting text into numerical representations that AI can understand.

*Storage: Saving the embeddings in a vector database.

*Retrieval: Searching the database to find information relevant to a user's question.

*Generation: Sending the retrieved information to the LLM to generate a useful response.

6. Important Technologies

Embeddings: Convert text into numbers that represent its meaning.

Vector Databases: Store and search information based on similarity in meaning.

APIs: Allow different applications and services to communicate with each other.

Chunking: Divides large documents into smaller sections to make information easier to process and retrieve.
